# Casegoods Intranet

A living knowledge base documenting the processes, workflows, and institutional memory of Casegoods: a furniture design and fabrication studio based in Mumbai.

This repository covers everything from design development and production to client communication, pricing, and sales. It exists to make Casegoods' way of working legible, transferable, and improvable over time.

---

## How to navigate

Browse by folder in the sidebar, or use the search bar to find anything by keyword. Notes are interlinked, follow the backlinks at the bottom of any page to discover related content.
