
For the art light the pendant and table versions come with an adjustable length cord. We have a standard length that's 1.2 m but if clients want something longer we can definitely accommodate it within an acceptable range, like up to 2.5 m or 3 m. It should be fine.

Apart from that the art light can come with a dimmable bulb. There are many types of bulbs:
- dimmable
- non-dimmable
- 5.5 W or 7.5 W
- 2700 K, 3000 K, or 4000 K


If the client wants to go into the specifics, they can choose the colour temperature they want. Typically we give for any 2700 K or 3000 K one with the 5.5 W light.

Also for the table version we can offer an in-line dimmer. Typically we place it based on our discretion but if the client wants it in a specific area, they can place it there as well. 

For the ceiling version it's important to know if it's a false ceiling, which is gypsum or plasterboard, or if it's a concrete ceiling. The concrete version is also called by many names: surface-mounted version, RCC version, etc. It comes with a fixed mount onto the ceiling and it's good to double-check with the client what the ceiling condition is before suggesting the price.

The wall versions are the least popular and least sold and we barely make them or have samples of them but that's how it is 

For finishes we offer:
- artlite in brass standard finishes (satin, slightly aged and blackened)
- aluminium (just satin, raw aluminium, neutral anodized aluminium, colour anodized)


You can ask Shailesh for the colours that are available. We also offer a brass nickel-plated version. Typically these used to be aluminium nickel-plated ones but we have since made it into brass nickel-plated because we've had poor results with aluminium nickel plating in the recent past. For most products you can take this as a thumb rule that aluminium nickel plating is generally a no-no.
