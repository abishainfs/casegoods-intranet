
Le mill

LeMill is a retailer in Apollo bunder that sells mostly clothes and fashion but also stocks furniture sometimes. They typically use it as a staging prop for their jewellery, products, small objects, and such but we never make much sales

The important thing is with LeMill to tell them that the price of anything that we quote to them excludes shipping and transport and all of that to the LeMill store. Alternatively if the pieces are at the store and we have to send those exact pieces to the client, we'll have to get it to a workshop, have it re finished, and then send it back to them. All of which is an additional cost that they'll have to bear. Typically they don't know this and they keep forgetting it so it's important to have it on record every time because they have been known to throw tantrums with regards to pricing as their customers are not there to make a big ticket sale in any case 

please also note that as of financial year 2026-7 we have not shared any price with LeMill and all of the prices will be quoted upon request. Except for small objects like The diyas, dipas, chulhas that we are willing to let go even if the price increases a bit later down the road, but only those we have shared and the furniture, lights, everything else is quoted on request 


LeMill's pricing, as you will see reflected in the pricing sheet, is a 30% discount on the XGST price. That's what it comes to. If you want to be super specific, the lemill price times 1.481 will be the ex GST selling price to customer. 

This kind of comes to a little more than a 30% discount but we usually round it off to 30. They are okay with it because they are paying more than what they would if they chose the actual discount price, I guess 

Do have a look at the whole pricing clarification document for more details on how things are priced


General items

They have also been using them as staging objects and we sell things to them on a consignment basis. They don't really pay the full price of the object that they're buying. It's just that only when they have an order, they will purchase it and then make the sale to the customer. It's very ad-hoc in that way.
