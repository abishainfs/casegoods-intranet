## Dipa Troubleshooting FAQ

#products, #objects #troubleshooting

If a client or retailer reaches out because their [[Dipa]] lamp (Tall or Wide) keeps extinguishing after just a few minutes, use this guide to walk them through the initial fixes.

## 🛠️ Suggested First Fixes

Before looking at technical replacements, have the client try these operational adjustments:
### 1. Wick Saturation & Prep
- **Give it a long soak:** Whenever using a brand-new wick, let it soak in a completely full lamp for a **good 10 minutes** before lighting it for the first time.

- **The "Fresh Pull":** Every single time the lamp is freshly lit, pull the wick out slightly. You can trim a tiny bit if it's heavily charred, though it's rarely necessary.

### 2. Wick Length & Positioning

- **Total Wick Length:** Ensure the total physical length of the wick inside the lamp matches our current workshop recommendations:
    - **Tall Dipa:** 80–100 mm
    - **Wide Dipa:** 50–70 mm

- **Exposed Wick Tip:** The tip of the wick extending out of the holder only needs to be about **3–4 mm**. A short tip can easily sustain a flame for over an hour.
- **Initial Flare:** Remind the client that a heightened flame is completely normal right at the start; the height stabilizes after a few minutes of burning.

> [!tip] **Wick Slipping?** If the wick accidentally slips down into the holder and smothers the flame, it can be gently lifted back up using a pair of metal tweezers.

### 3. Oil Type & Volume

- **Fill it up completely:** Dipas burn significantly better and longer when they are filled entirely to the top with oil.
- **Switch the oil:** Flame quality and burning behavior differ wildly across different oils. We strongly recommend high-quality **paraffin lamp oil** or a local equivalent. Avoid heavy cooking/vegetable oils if they are causing clogging.

## 🛑 If Those Don't Work: What to Do Next

If the client has tried all the variations of oil and soaking and the lamp still fails to stay lit, it points to a known product issue. Here is the operational protocol:

### Step 1: Check the Nozzle Version

Older production batches used an earlier nozzle design that restricted oil flow. We later developed a new version with improved flow. If it's a legacy client, we can check if sending replacement nozzles helps. Ask Kenneth for clarification on this, and the workshop can help send it over.

### Step 2: The Core Product Policy (Discontinuation)

Because these flow issues persisted across international local oils, **the Dipa has been officially discontinued from the Casegoods collection**.

> [!warning] **Handling Retailer Inquiries** Do not try to push or re-sell the Dipa if a retailer says it isn't functioning properly. Instead, issue a **store credit** equal to the wholesale price they paid. They are free to retain the physical pieces in their inventory, but we recommend they stop selling them to end customers.

### Step 3: Pivot to Alternatives

When wrapping up the issue and applying the store credit, warmly steer the client toward our reliable alternatives:

- **[[Buenos Diyas]] (Large or Small):** Traditional oil lamps that include extra wicks in the box and don't suffer from flow issues.
    
- **[[Chulha Oil Diffuser]]:** A great alternative that runs cleanly on standard tea light candles (one candle included in the box).
