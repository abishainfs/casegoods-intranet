## Lighting Specifications & Customization Guidelines tags: [products, lighting, specifications, operations]

## Lighting Order Requirements Checklist

Before finalizing any lighting order for the [[Art Light]] or [[Dome Up-light]], use this checklist to confirm the technical details with the client.

- **Essential (Required before quoting/production):**
    
    - [ ] **Ceiling Condition:** Is it a false ceiling (gypsum/plasterboard) or concrete (RCC/surface-mounted)? _Crucial for pricing the ceiling version._
        
    - [ ] **Cord Length:** Is the standard 1.2 m length sufficient, or do they need an extension (up to 2.5 m or 3 m)?
        
    - [ ] **Bulb Preferences:** Do they require dimmable or non-dimmable? Specific color temperature (2700K / 3000K / 4000K)?
        
    - [ ] **Dome Up-light Configuration:** 2-bulb or 3-bulb variation?
        
- **Flexible (Can be determined during production design):**
    
    - [ ] **In-line Dimmer Placement:** For table versions, does the client want the dimmer switch in a specific spot, or are they leaving it to our discretion?
        

## Art Light Configurations

### Cord Lengths (Pendant & Table Versions)

- **Standard Offering:** Comes with a **1.2 m** adjustable length cord.
    
- **Custom Range:** If a client requests a longer cord, we can accommodate extensions up to **2.5 m or 3 m**.

### Bulb Options & Technical Details

We offer a high degree of flexibility with bulb selections. If a client wants to dive into the technical specifics, they can choose from the following matrix:

- **Dimmability:** Dimmable or Non-dimmable
    
- **Wattage:** 5.5 W or 7.5 W
    
- **Color Temperature:** 2700 K, 3000 K, or 4000 K
    

> 💡 **Default Configuration Standard:** If the client does not specify technical preferences, our standard issue is a **5.5 W bulb at either 2700 K or 3000 K**. Full technical spec sheets for these can be found in the team Dropbox by searching for **"OSRAM bulb"**.

### Dimmer Placement (Table Version)

- The table version can be equipped with an **in-line dimmer**.
    
- **Placement:** By default, we place this switch along the cord based on our design discretion. However, always ask the client if they have a specific spatial requirement for where the switch should sit.
    

### Ceiling Variations & Installation Types

It is absolutely critical to verify the client's ceiling type **before** finalizing quotes for the ceiling-mounted versions:

1. **False Ceiling:** Installed into gypsum or plasterboard.
    
2. **Concrete Ceiling:** Also referred to as **RCC version** or **Surface-Mounted version**. This configuration uses a fixed mount directly onto the concrete.
    

> ⚠️ **Pricing Guardrail:** Always double-check the ceiling condition with the client first, as the mounting hardware changes the cost structure.

### Wall Version Status

- The wall-mounted [[Art Light]] is our least popular and least sold variant.
    
- We rarely fabricate these and do not regularly keep physical samples of them in the studio. Keep this in mind if a client asks to see one in person.
    

## Dome Up-light Configurations

The bulb specification flexibility outlined above also applies directly to the [[Dome Up-light]].

- **Bulb Quantity Options:** This product can be customized to hold either a **2-bulb** or **3-bulb** configuration.
    
- **Technical Specs:** The light output and temperature can be custom-selected based on the same OSRAM technical sheets available in the Dropbox folder, which can be shared directly with the client if requested.
    

## Material Finishes & Production Thumb Rules

### Brass Finishes (Standard)

Available for the [[Art Light]] in our standard studio treatments:

- Satin
    
- Slightly Aged
    
- Blackened
    

### Aluminium Finishes

Available in the following variants:

- Satin / Raw Aluminium
    
- Neutral Anodised
    
- Colour Anodised _(Note: Check directly with Shailesh to see which specific colors are currently available in the workshop before promising a tone to a client)._
    

### 🚫 The Aluminium Nickel-Plating Thumb Rule

- **Never offer or accept orders for aluminium nickel plating.** We have had poor, inconsistent results replicating this finish in the recent past.
    
- **The Solution:** Across almost all Casegoods products, take it as a strict rule that we use **brass nickel plating** instead. If a client asks for a silver/nickel look, guide them away from aluminium and confidently offer the brass nickel-plated version instead.
