


## Art Light Order Requirements Checklist

Before processing or finalizing an order for the [[Art Light]], use this quick checklist to track client details and technical requirements.

- **Essential (Required to lock in production/pricing):**
    
    - [ ] **Installation Type:** False ceiling (gypsum/plasterboard) vs. Concrete ceiling (RCC/Surface-mounted) _[Ceiling version only]_
        
    - [ ] **Finish Selection:** Confirmed material and treatment (e.g., Satin Brass, Blackened Brass)
        
    - [ ] **Cord Length:** Confirmed if standard 1.2 m is sufficient or if an extension is needed (up to 3 m)
        
- **Flexible (Can be determined later):**
    
    - [ ] **Bulb Specifics:** Dimmable vs. non-dimmable, wattage, and color temperature
        
    - [ ] **Dimmer Placement:** Specific placement on the cord vs. workshop discretion _[Table version only]_
        

## How to Use This Note

> **Note for Nekshan:** This is a modular email builder designed specifically for the [[Art Light]] (and related lighting inquiries). Copy the **Opening Block**, choose the **Content Modules** that match what you need to clarify with the client, and close with the **Closing Block**.

## The Email Builder

### Opening Block (Always Include)

> Dear [Client Name],
> 
> It's lovely to hear from you again! To finalize your order, could you please confirm a few technical details and your preferred finishes for the lights?

### Module A: Finish Selection & Stock Check

_Use this to get finish confirmation and flag that we may already have stock ready to allocate._

> Could you please confirm your desired finish for the [[Art Light]]? You'll find our complete finish swatches in the attached specifications sheet. We occasionally have select configurations in stock, so once you confirm your finish, we can check our inventory to see if we can fast-track your order.
> 
> As a reminder, our standard options include:
> 
> - **Brass:** Satin, Slightly Aged, or Blackened Brass
>     
> - **Aluminium:** Satin/Raw or Neutral Anodised
>     
> - **Plated:** Brass Nickel-Plated
>     

> 💡 **Tip for Nekshan (Finishes Appendage):** > * **The Nickel Rule:** If they ask for a silver/nickel finish, _never_ offer aluminium nickel-plating (we have poor results replicating it). Always offer **brass nickel-plated** instead.
> 
> - **Anodised Colours:** If they want a custom colour anodised aluminium, double-check what is currently available in the workshop with Shailesh before confirming.
>     
> - **Co-ordering with [[Rolling Round Light]]:** If the client is cross-inquiring about the [[Rolling Round Light]], use this exact phrasing: _"For the Rolling Round Light, we currently don't offer a polished brass option. Do note that our dark brass finish presents as an almost-black initially, which lightens into a beautiful dark brass with time."_
>     

### Module B: Ceiling Condition Verification

_Crucial module to include if the client is ordering the ceiling/pendant version. Do not skip this, as it changes hardware and installation pricing._

> For the ceiling-mounted version of the [[Art Light]], could you please let us know the structural condition of your ceiling?
> 
> - **False Ceiling:** Gypsum or plasterboard setup.
>     
> - **Concrete Ceiling:** Also known as an RCC or Surface-Mounted setup, which requires a fixed, visible mount directly onto the concrete.
>     
> 
> Once we confirm your ceiling type, we can ensure we supply the correct mounting hardware.

### Module C: Cord Length Customization

_Use this if the client is ordering a pendant or table version and has high ceilings or specific spatial constraints._

> The pendant and table versions of the [[Art Light]] come standard with a **1.2 m adjustable cord**. If your space requires a longer drop, we can easily accommodate custom lengths up to **2.5 m or 3 m** within an acceptable structural range. Please let us know if you require an extension.

### Module D: Bulb & Dimmer Specifications

_Use this if the client wants to customize their lighting output or has specific electrical automation/dimming requirements._

> Our lights offer great flexibility when it comes to electrical configurations. Our standard default configuration is a **5.5 W bulb at either 2700 K or 3000 K**, but if your space requires specific illumination, you can select from the options below:
> 
> - **Dimmability:** Dimmable or Non-dimmable options available.
>     
> - **Wattage:** 5.5 W or 7.5 W profiles.
>     
> - **Color Temperature:** 2700 K (warm), 3000 K (soft white), or 4000 K (cool white).
>     
> 
> _For Table Versions:_ We can also include an **in-line dimmer switch** along the cord. We typically position this based on our design discretion, but please let us know if you have a specific spot on the cord where you would prefer the switch to sit.

> 💡 **Tip for Nekshan:** If a client asks for highly technical datasheets for these bulbs, search the team Dropbox for **"OSRAM bulb"** and send over the PDF. This exact bulb flexibility can also be offered for the [[Dome Up-light]] (which comes in 2-bulb or 3-bulb configurations).
