### Module C: Explaining the "Standard" Sizing Math

_Use this if a client is confused about how individual bay tiers add up to the overall shelf height._

> To clarify, when we refer to a "standard configuration," it means the [[Hold Shelf - Comms Template]] comes with **two bays (or tiers)**. The individual heights of these tiers can be mixed and matched within our standard module options.
> 
> The **900 mm height** mentioned in our price list refers to a typical example setup. When purchasing, you select the specific height for each tier. The total height is calculated by adding the tier components, the feet, and the metal sheets together.
> 
> **Example Configuration 1 (The Standard 900mm Look):**
> 
> - Bottom Tier: 450 mm
>     
> - Top Tier: 350 mm
>     
> - Base Feet: 82 mm
>     
> - Metal Sheets (3 layers total): 18 mm
>     
> - **Total Profile Height:** 900 mm
>     
> 
> **Example Configuration 2 (Low Profile):**
> 
> - Bottom Tier: 275 mm
>     
> - Top Tier: 250 mm
>     
> - Base Feet: 82 mm
>     
> - Metal Sheets (3 layers total): 18 mm
>     
> - **Total Profile Height:** 625 mm
>     
> 
> I hope this breaks down how the modular system accumulates height!

> 💡 **Tip for Nekshan:** Clients frequently forget to account for the **82 mm feet** and the **18 mm sheet thickness** when trying to fit shelves into tight architectural spaces. Keep this breakdown handy to audit their math before sending orders to the workshop.
