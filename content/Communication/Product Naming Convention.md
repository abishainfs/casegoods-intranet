## Scope

This document governs how product names are written across all internal and external [[Communication]]. It addresses naming only.

---
## 1. Product Name Structures
  
Casegoods products follow one of two formats:

**A. Standalone Product**

Format: Proper Product Name
Examples: Hold Shelf, Right Triangle Table, Post Lamp, Dipa Oil Lamps, Three Cylinder Table.
Usage: the Hold Shelf, the Right Triangle Table.
Incorrect: hold shelf, Right triangle table.

**B. Series + Variant**

Format: Series Name + Product Type
Examples: Drift Dining Chair, Drift Lounge Chair, Drift Bar Stool, Drift Side Table.
Rules:
- Series name always capitalised.
- Product type capitalised when attached to series name.
- No hyphens, brackets, or possessives.
    Correct: Drift Dining Chair, the Drift Lounge Chair.
    Incorrect: Drift dining chair, Drift – Dining Chair, Drift (Dining Chair), Drift’s Dining Chair.

---
## 2. Capitalisation, Plurals & Repetition

**First Reference** On first mention, always use the complete product name.

- The Drift Dining Chair is available in teak and oak.
- The Hold Shelf can be configured with additional bays.
- The Standing Straight Lamp is available in multiple marble options.

This mirrors the approach used by brands such as Knoll, where the official product name is consistently used in titles and introductory copy. Example: The [Knoll Cesca Chair](https://www.knoll.com/design-plan/product/cesca?section=design&utm_source=chatgpt.com)⁠ is introduced using its complete product name before subsequent references become more informal.

**Subsequent References** After the first mention, a generic noun may be used where the meaning is unambiguous.

**Correct**

- The Drift Dining Chair is available in teak and oak. The chair is offered in two finishes.
- The Hold Shelf can be configured with additional bays. The shelf may be installed freestanding or wall-mounted.
- The Standing Straight Lamp is available in four marble options. The lamp is assembled to order.

---

## 3. Materials & Descriptors

Materials and finishes are **not part of the product name**. They are descriptors.

Correct:
- Drift Dining Chair in teak
- Hold Shelf in black anodised aluminium
- Right Triangle Table in all-teak finish

Incorrect:
- Drift Teak Dining Chair
- Teak Right Triangle Table
- Black Hold Shelf

If referring to the series as a whole:

- the Drift series
- the Drift collection (avoid collection - it's a more casual noun )
    (Not Drift Series unless used as a title header.)

---

## 4. Edge Cases

**Geometry or Descriptive Names**
Maintain exact wording: Right Triangle Table, Three Cylinder Table.
Do not reorder: Triangle Right Table.

**Functional Words in Official Names**
If part of the official name, capitalise: Post Lamp, Dipa Oil Lamps. If generic reference: the lamp, the oil lamps.

**Internal Abbreviations (Internal Use Only)**
DDC = Drift Dining Chair
RTL = Right Triangle Table

Never use abbreviations in client-facing communication.

---

## Naming Formula Summary

Standalone → Proper Name (Hold Shelf)
Series Product → Series + Variant (Drift Dining Chair)
Materials → Always separate descriptor
Plural → Final noun only

If unsure:

1. Is this a standalone product?    
2. Is this a series + variant?
    Apply structure exactly and consistently.
